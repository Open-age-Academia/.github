# Open-Age Academia

**We help unaffiliated young researchers publish their work and secure research funding.**

Research should be judged on its merits, not on the age of its author. But submission systems, conference policies, and funding rules often assume an affiliation and an age that serious young researchers, including high schoolers, may not have. We work to lower those barriers.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/openageacademia.org-ED702D?style=flat-square)](https://openageacademia.org)
[![Email](https://img.shields.io/badge/contact@openageacademia.org-333333?style=flat-square)](mailto:contact@openageacademia.org)

### What we do

- **[Open venues](https://openageacademia.org/venues)**: a running list of venues and programs that accept submissions without an institutional email or supervisor sign-off, with documented workarounds for those that don't.
- **[Stories](https://openageacademia.org/story)**: first-hand accounts of doing research without an affiliation: what got in the way, and how people got past it.

### Get involved

- Know a venue that welcomes unaffiliated researchers? Email us and we'll add it.
- Did research on your own? [Share your story](https://openageacademia.org/story).
